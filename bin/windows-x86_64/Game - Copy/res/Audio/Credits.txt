Audio credits

Every sound here was trimmed, loudness normalized and re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis for the game. Most
sound effects were folded to mono. Music loops had the frozen samples at their end removed and a
short crossfade into the loop start added (LOOPSTART/LOOPLENGTH tags); ambience loops were
crossfaded into themselves.
